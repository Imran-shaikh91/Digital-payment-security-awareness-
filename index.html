ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Awareness is your first layer of protection.

This educational website explains common digital-payment risks and safer practices. It is designed for awareness and learning. It does not connect to banks, process payments or collect financial credentials.

MODULE 01

Understand digital payments

Know what happens when you pay digitally.

📱

UPI payments

UPI lets users transfer money between bank accounts through supported apps. Always check the recipient details before approving a payment.

🏦

Banking apps

Use official banking applications and keep your phone, apps and operating system updated.

💳

Cards & wallets

Protect card details and wallet access. Enable transaction alerts where available and review account activity regularly.

MODULE 02

Essential safety practices

Make these checks part of every transaction.

01

Verify the receiver

Check the recipient's name and payment details shown in your payment app before confirming.

02

Never share secrets

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password, CVV or full card details with anyone. Genuine support staff should not ask for your PIN or OTP.

03

Remember how PINs work

Entering a UPI PIN generally authorises a debit from your account. You do not need to enter a PIN just to receive money.

04

Check the transaction status

Confirm payment inside the app's transaction history or bank statement. Do not rely only on a screenshot or message.

05

Be careful with links

Avoid unknown links, QR codes and screen-sharing requests. Open your payment app directly instead of following unexpected messages.

06

Secure your device

Use a screen lock, install apps from trusted app stores, update software and avoid payments on public or shared devices.

MODULE 03

Recognise common warning signs

Pause when a request creates urgency or asks for confidential information.

🎁

Prize or reward message

A message claims you won a prize and asks you to pay a fee or share an OTP. Treat unexpected rewards with suspicion.

📞

Impersonation call

A caller pretends to be bank or customer support and pressures you to reveal a PIN, OTP or password. End the call and contact the institution using its official channel.

🔗

Fake payment link

An unknown link asks you to sign in, install an app or approve a request. Do not proceed; verify independently.

MODULE 06

What to do if you suspect fraud

  1. Contact your bank or payment service provider immediately using its official app, website or phone number.
  2. For financial cyber fraud in India, call the national helpline 1930 as soon as possible.
  3. Report the incident through the Government of India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  4. Keep transaction references and relevant messages for the bank or authorities. Never post account details or OTPs publicly.

This website is an educational project, not an emergency service. Follow the latest instructions from official authorities and your bank.
